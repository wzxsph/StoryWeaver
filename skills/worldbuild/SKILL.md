# StoryWeaver Worldbuild

Use this skill when the user asks to create, expand, repair, or systematize a novel setting.

## Goal

Produce a usable story bible, not decorative lore. Every setting element should help chapters generate better conflict, clearer stakes, more consistent rules, or stronger reader expectations.

## Workflow

1. Identify genre, target reader promise, core fantasy, protagonist pressure, and long-term escalation path.
2. Define hard world rules and costs before exceptions.
3. Create only the organizations, scenes, concepts, and items needed for the next planning horizon.
4. Mark unstable ideas as `draft` and locked canon as `locked`.
5. Save structured outputs into `state/metadata/project.json`, `state/concepts/`, `state/organizations/`, `state/scenes/`, `state/items/`, and `state/outline/outline.json` as appropriate.
6. Record open questions separately instead of pretending uncertain details are canon.

## Best Practices

- Tie every power system to cost, limitation, social consequence, and visible escalation.
- Give every major faction a resource, fear, public face, private agenda, and conflict with at least one other faction.
- Define scenes by function in story: refuge, battlefield, market, mystery box, threshold, prison, temptation, or revelation.
- Keep names, ranks, currencies, and measurements consistent once introduced.
